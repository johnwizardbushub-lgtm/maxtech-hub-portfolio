## Review focus

Compare spend and response across ad sets, reconcile recorded lead events and identify where continued testing needs clearer tracking. One competitor-offer ad set recorded two leads at £10.35 spend, approximately £5.18 per lead.

## Interpretation

The two-lead ad-set result is a small sample. It does not establish a repeatable acquisition cost or justify a guaranteed outcome. The audit is evidence of performance review and lead reconciliation, not proof of profitable customer acquisition.
